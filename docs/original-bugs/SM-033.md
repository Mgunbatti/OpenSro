# SM-033: A customizing drop of group 16 is written over the monster's Except columns

- Original: `CRefInstanceGenerator::LoadCustomizingReservedItemDrop` (0x4a57d0), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `RefDrop_LoadCustomizingReservedItemDrop` in
  `SilkroadOnline/Server/ServerCommon/RefDataDrop.cpp`
- Severity: wrong

## What the original does

A _RefCustomizingReservedItemDropForMonster row accepts a GroupID of 0..16 and appends the entry
to the vector at RefObjChar +0x2A8 + 16 * GroupID. RefObjChar has only 16 such vectors (ctor
0x4a3040 / dtor 0x4a3110), so group 16 is the memory at +0x3A8: Except[0] and Except[1] of the
_RefObjChar row, read before. The push_back treats those two columns as a vector.

## Effect

The Except_1 / Except_2 values of the monster are replaced by heap pointers (or the push_back
writes through whatever the columns held); the entries are never freed and the group is never
found by the drop code.

## Fix

Reject a GroupID above 15 (or give the monster 17 vectors).
