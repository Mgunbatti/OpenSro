# SM-164: Random arena team keeps the level of the removed member

- Original: `CBattleArenaMgr::StartGameRandom` (0x6723b0), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: the same function in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/BattleArena.cpp`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

When the last random team has an odd number of members, its first member is removed with
`sBattleArenaTeam::PopMember` and kicked (reason 6). The level sum of the team (`+0x14`) still
holds the level of that member; the level bracket of the team is then computed as
`Arena_GetLevelBracket(level sum / member count)`.

## Effect

The average level of the last team is too high by up to one level per member, which can move
the team into a higher level bracket. The bracket decides whether the team needs 4 or 8 players
to start and is sent to the game servers with 0x34D2 type 16.

## Fix

Subtract the level of the removed member from the level sum.
