# SM-020: The silk price check of the package items never fails

- Original: `RefPrice_HasOnlyAllowedCurrencies` (0x49f7e0), called by `RefPackageItem::ValidateCost` (0x495650), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `RefPrice_HasOnlyAllowedCurrencies` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ServerCommon/RefDataShop.cpp`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

Before a `_RefPricePolicyOfItem` price is stored, the prices a package item already has are
checked: for every currency kind 0..4 except 2 the check fails when all prices are of kind 2
*and* all prices are of that other kind. "All prices are of kind k" also requires at least one
price, so both conditions can never hold at once and the check always passes.

## Effect

The "Remove all price of (%s) except silkprice suit." message and the `exit(0)` behind it are
dead code: a package item can mix any currencies.

## Fix

Test the intended combination (presumably kind 2 together with any other kind, i.e. an `||` of
"has a kind 2 price" and "has a price of kind k").
