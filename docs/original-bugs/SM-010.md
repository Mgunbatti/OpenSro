# SM-010: Every _RefGameWorldNPC row leaks

- Original: `CRefInstanceGenerator::LoadGameWorldNPC` (0x4a63f0), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `RefData_LoadGameWorldNPC` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ServerCommon/ReferenceData.cpp`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

The loader allocates a 108-byte row, reads it and pushes a copy of the part after the key into
the vector of its key in the map. The allocated row itself is freed only when the reader rejects
it; after a successful copy it is dropped without being freed.

## Effect

Each row of the table leaks once per start-up (108 bytes plus its two strings).

## Fix

Free the row after copying it into the vector (or keep it on the stack).
