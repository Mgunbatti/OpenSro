# FW11-025: CSchedule_Repeat_Sometime puts the running slot after the current time

- Original: `NewSchedule::CSchedule_Repeat_Sometime::CheckCurrentPeriod` (0x6dbf30), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `CSchedule_Repeat_Sometime::CheckCurrentPeriod` in `JMX_Library/BSLib/NewSchedule/Schedule.cpp`
- Severity: medium (wrong times for repeating schedules that are running at service start)

## What the original does

`GetCurrentPeriod` computes how many minutes the time lies inside its slot (`nOffset`) and passes them to `CheckCurrentPeriod`, which builds the slot begin as minute + nOffset instead of minute - nOffset (the end as begin + length - nOffset), and the next slot from that begin.

## Effect

For a slot of 10 minutes every 30 minutes, 10:35 gives the running period 10:40..10:45 and the next one 11:10..11:20 instead of 10:30..10:40 and 11:00..11:10. Only times exactly on a slot start are right.

## Fix

Subtract the offset.
