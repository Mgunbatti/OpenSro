# FM-002: the 0x6005 query of a session without cord lists every unreachable body

- Original: `CServerProcessMain::OnMsg_2001` (0x4029c0), FarmManager (2007 framework)
- Rewrite: `CServerProcessMain::OnMsg_2001` in
  `JMX_ServerFramework/ServerFramework/ServerProcessMain.cpp` (`JMX_FRAMEWORK_2007`)
- Severity: low

## What the original does

After a peer's introduction (0x2001) the 2007 framework asks the peer for the states of the
bodies behind it (0x6005): it lists every other body whose route (`ServerBody_Find`) is the cord
of the session, and then their cords. When the module is known but has no cord to this server,
the cord is NULL, and every body without a route compares equal to it.

## Effect

Such a peer is asked for the states of all bodies this server cannot reach, and their cords.
The answer (0x2005) updates the state of bodies that have nothing to do with that peer.

## Fix

Send the body list only when there is a cord (the 2008 framework writes the cord's peer body
only, and nothing without a cord).
