# SM-009: Siege rows out of service are loaded

- Original: `CRefInstanceGenerator::LoadSiegeFortressGuard` (0x4a4b50),
  `LoadSiegeFortressItemForge` (0x4a4bf0), `LoadSiegeLvlSummonMonster` (0x4a4f10),
  `LoadSiegeFortressRewards` (0x4a50b0), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `RefData_LoadSiegeByFortress`, `RefData_LoadSiegeLvlSummonMonster`,
  `RefData_LoadSiegeFortressRewards` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ServerCommon/ReferenceData.cpp`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

The four tables have a Service column like the other siege tables, but their loaders copy the
record and store the row by its key (FortressID / RefOrgObjID) without looking at Service.
The fortress rewards are stored even without a FortressID.

## Effect

Switching a guard, item forge recipe, summoned monster level or fortress reward off in the
database has no effect; the row is used anyway.

## Fix

Skip (and free) rows whose Service column is 0, as the other siege loaders do.
