# SM-277: A reused vote keeps the tally of the previous vote

- Original: `CVote::Reset` (0x4612d0), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `CVote::Reset` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/Vote.cpp`
- Severity: major

## What the original does

Votes come from a pool. `Reset` (on allocation by `CreateVote` and on release) clears the IDs,
the ballot map and the candidate vector (+0x40), but not the tally vector (+0x30) that holds the
votes per candidate. The ballot map is also emptied without returning the ballots to their pool
unless `ClearResults` ran before.

## Effect

From the second use of a pooled vote on, `AddChoice` asserts (the two vectors differ in size)
and appends behind the old counts, so candidate N is counted in the slot of the previous vote's
candidate N: a master vote can be decided at once by the counts left from an earlier vote, or
never reach 60 percent. Ballots of votes that end without `ClearResults` leak from their pool.

## Fix

Clear the tally in `Reset` and release the ballots before the map is emptied.
