# AS-004: XTrap_S_SetActiveCode is not checked after loading the DLL

- Original: `sub_40DE70` (XTrap_LoadDll) and `sub_401110` (XTrap_Initialize), AgentServer
- Rewrite: `XTrap_LoadDll` / `XTrap_Initialize` in `JMX_CommonServers/AgentServer/XTrap.cpp`
- Severity: low

## What the original does

`XTrap_LoadDll` looks up thirteen procedures of XTrap4Server.dll and reports success when eleven
of them were found; `XTrap_S_SetActiveCode` and `XTrap_S_SaveLog` are not part of the check.
`XTrap_Initialize` calls `XTrap_S_SetActiveCode` right after a successful `XTrap_S_Start`.

## Effect

A DLL without `XTrap_S_SetActiveCode` makes the server call address 0 while it starts.

## Fix

Check `XTrap_S_SetActiveCode` with the others (and treat a missing one as a load failure).
