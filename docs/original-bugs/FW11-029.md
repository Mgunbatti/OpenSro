# FW11-029: CStringCheck::ParseWords copies words into a fixed buffer without a limit

- Original: `CStringCheck::ParseWords` (0x6df660), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `CStringCheck::ParseWords` in `JMX_Library/BSLib/Util/StringCheck.cpp`
- Severity: low (names are short; the stack buffer holds 1024 characters)

## What the original does

Every space-separated word of the checked name is copied character by character into a 1024-byte stack buffer until the next space or the end, without a bound.

## Effect

A word of 1024 characters or more overwrites the stack (the /GS cookie ends the process).

## Fix

Bound the copy to the buffer size.
