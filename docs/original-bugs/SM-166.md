# SM-166: Job arena team without members divides by zero

- Original: `CBattleArenaMgr::StartGameJob` (0x674120), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: the same function in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ShardManager/BattleArena.cpp`
- Severity: critical

## What the original does

Every job team is finished with `Arena_GetLevelBracket(level sum / member count)` without
checking the member count. The other arena kinds check it first. A job team stays empty when
`OpenArenaLayer` fails (all its players are kicked with reason 7 instead of joining) or when all
of its players went offline or left character selection since the check.

## Effect

The unsigned division by zero raises an integer divide exception in the shard manager's main
thread; the process crashes at the start of the job arena game.

## Fix

Skip the team (or leave the bracket 0) when it has no members, like the random, party and guild
versions.
