# SM-008: A reference row out of service leaks

- Original: `CRefInstanceGenerator::LoadSiegeBlessBuff` (0x4a5170), `LoadSiegeDungeon`
  (0x4a5250), `LoadSiegeQuest` (0x4a5330), `LoadSiegeQuestReward` (0x4a53e0), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `RefData_LoadSiegeUnique`, `RefData_LoadSiegeQuest`, `RefData_LoadSiegeQuestReward` in
  `SilkroadOnline/Server/ServerCommon/ReferenceData.cpp`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

The loader allocates the parsed row and copies the record into it. When the Service column is 0
it returns success without storing the row and without freeing it (the other loaders free it).

## Effect

Every row out of service in these tables leaks its row once per start-up.

## Fix

Free the row when it is out of service.
