# SM-022: The shop goods are never freed

- Original: `CRefInstanceGenerator::LoadShopTabAndGoods` (0x4a3940) / `RefShopTab::DestroyMembers` (0x5166b0), SR_ShardManager
- Rewrite: `RefShop_LoadShopTabAndGoods` in `SilkroadOnline/Server/ServerCommon/RefDataShop.cpp`
- Severity: minor

## What the original does

Each `_RefShopGoods` row is allocated and stored in the 240 slots of its tab
(`RefShopTab::AssignGoods`). Nothing else keeps it, and the tab's destructor frees only the slot
vector, not the goods in it. A row stored over a taken slot (after the assert) is lost at once.

## Effect

All goods rows (204 bytes plus three strings each) leak when the reference data is destroyed at
shutdown.

## Fix

Let the tab free its goods in its destructor.
