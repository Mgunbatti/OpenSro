# MM-002: started server processes leak two handles

- Original: `CMachineManagerMainProcess::OnMsg_6301_StartServerProcess` (0x401470), MachineManager
- Rewrite: `CMachineManagerMainProcess::OnMsg_6301` in
  `JMX_CommonServers/MachineManager/MachineManagerMainProcess.cpp`
- Severity: low

## What the original does

0x6301 starts `<module dir>\<module name>.exe` with `CreateProcessA`. The process and thread
handles returned in `PROCESS_INFORMATION` are never closed.

## Effect

Every successful start keeps two handles open in MachineManager, and the kernel objects of the
started process stay alive after it has ended until MachineManager exits. With modules restarted
often over a long uptime the handle count grows without bound.

## Fix

Close `pi.hThread` and `pi.hProcess` after a successful `CreateProcessA`.
